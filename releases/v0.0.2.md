**Second test build**, based on feedback from 0.0.1.

### Download
**Recommended:** `ASTFC-Voice-Protocol-0.0.2-Setup.exe` below (Assets). Run it; it installs for your account (no admin) and replaces 0.0.1. Your settings are kept.
If the app won't start, send Jeff `crash.log` from `%APPDATA%\ASTFC Voice Protocol`.

### What's new
- **Softer High Contrast.** 0.0.1's version was "definitely high contrast, but too bright". It now uses near-black, light gray text, gray outlines and calmer cyan/amber. All text still meets the strictest contrast level (WCAG AAA, 7:1). The old pure black-and-white look is still available as **Maximum Contrast** in Settings.
- **Microphone and Discord.** If Windows takes the microphone away, the app now notices and reconnects by itself. If it hears no sound for 20 seconds, the dashboard tells you how to fix it. There's also a new **Reconnect microphone** button.

### If the app hears nothing while Discord is open
1. Windows Sound settings › **More sound settings** › **Recording** tab.
2. Right-click your headset mic › **Set as Default Device**.
3. Same mic › **Properties** › **Advanced**: untick both **"Allow applications to take exclusive control"** boxes › OK.
4. In the app, press **Reconnect microphone**.

See `Commands 0.0.2.txt` (also in the Start menu after installing) for what you can say.
